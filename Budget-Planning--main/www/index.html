About Jehulie Budget Planner

Jehulie Budget Planner is a user-friendly personal finance tool designed to help individuals take control of their money with ease. At its core, the app lets users track all sources of income, log every expense, and automatically calculate the net balance—giving a clear, real-time snapshot of financial health, as seen in the dashboard where total amounts are displayed prominently.

Features include:

• A streamlined interface for quickly adding transactions, with fields for description, amount, and type (Income/Expense). ​

• Instant confirmation when transactions are successfully recorded, along with dynamic updates to the main financial summary. ​

• Options to filter data by month and year, enabling users to review spending and earning patterns over specific periods. ​

• A dedicated transactions section to view, manage, and reference all financial entries, with easy access via the "View All" button.

Built for simplicity and efficiency, the app eliminates complexity so users can focus on budgeting basics—planning wisely, staying on top of cash flow, and working toward their financial goals without hassle..
